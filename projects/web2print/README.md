# Web-to-Print Product Ecosystem

## Overview

Contributed to the development, customization, and delivery of a comprehensive Web-to-Print product ecosystem serving international printing businesses.

The ecosystem consists of three major product lines:

- A Web-to-Print plugin for WooCommerce
- A SaaS Web-to-Print platform built with React and Next.js
- An embeddable design platform powered by Node.js, allowing online design capabilities to be integrated into third-party eCommerce websites

My role focused on understanding customer business requirements, proposing practical software solutions, coordinating implementation, and ensuring successful project delivery.

## Product Ecosystem

### 1. WooCommerce Web-to-Print Plugin

A Web-to-Print plugin built for WooCommerce, enabling online product personalization, design customization, and print-ready order generation.

A cloud-based Web-to-Print platform developed with React and Next.js, allowing businesses to register, manage their stores, and provide online product design services without managing their own infrastructure.

A Node.js-based design platform that enables online product personalization to be embedded into external eCommerce websites through APIs and SDKs.
